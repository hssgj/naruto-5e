# Naruto 5e — Core Combat Rules v0.1

**Status:** PLAYABLE DRAFT — rules anchor

## Core principle

Naruto flavor changes what an action looks like, not how many actions a creature receives.

Unless a feature explicitly says otherwise, use normal 5e action economy:
- 1 Action
- 1 Bonus Action, if available
- 1 Reaction per round
- Movement up to speed
- Free object interaction / speech as normal

A jutsu does not automatically grant extra attacks, movement, reactions or turns merely because the fiction sounds fast.

## Rolls

For every uncertain meaningful action, the GM states the check/attack/save before resolving it when practical.

Default resolution uses the established Naruto 5e framework and the technique's defined method.

### Advantage / disadvantage
Roll two d20s and take the higher/lower result. Multiple sources do not stack. One advantage and one disadvantage cancel.

### Natural 20 / natural 1
For attack rolls, natural 20 is a critical hit and natural 1 is a miss, following 5e. For ability checks and saving throws, a natural 20/1 has no automatic universal success/failure unless explicitly adopted.

## Action economy

A normal combat turn may contain:
- Movement
- One Action
- One Bonus Action if a feature allows it
- One Reaction per round
- Free interaction/speech

Multiple jutsu in one turn require a mechanical reason, such as Action + Bonus Action, Action + Reaction, Extra Attack with compatible attacks, or a feature specifically permitting it.

Rapid hand seals are flavor unless a technique's mechanics say otherwise.

## Reactions

A reaction is a real resource. Once spent, it is unavailable until the creature's next turn begins.

A defensive technique is a Reaction only when the character has a defined reaction feature/jutsu allowing it.

## Jutsu construction

Every recurring jutsu should eventually have:
- Name
- Nature/affinity
- Action cost
- Range
- Target/area
- Attack/save/check
- Damage/effect
- Duration
- Concentration requirement, if any
- Chakra cost
- Scaling
- Special interactions

Improvised one-off jutsu can exist without a full stat block, but the GM assigns a temporary cost and effect before resolution.

## Chakra

Chakra is the Naruto resource layer over 5e. It must have an explicit pool and spending rules before use.

Do not silently treat every successful jutsu as free.

Exact recovery and progression rules remain unresolved where not explicitly locked.

## Nature Energy / Senjutsu

Nature Energy is separate from ordinary Chakra. It does not automatically increase every roll or damage value.

Until a formal Senjutsu subsystem is established, uncertain numerical interactions must be marked DRAFT or handled as a clearly declared provisional ruling.

## Summoning

A summoning jutsu creates the summon physically in the world unless the specific technique says otherwise.

A summon needs an explicit action cost, range, duration and Chakra cost before those values are treated as fixed mechanics.

## Substitution

Substitution is a defensive technique, not an unlimited dodge. If used as a Reaction, the character must have an established Reaction-based substitution rule and satisfy its trigger/requirements.

## Terrain manipulation

Doton can create cover, difficult terrain, walls, pits, pillars and similar structures. Area and duration must be declared. Large narrative destruction does not automatically become huge combat damage.

## Concentration and conditions

Where a jutsu is mechanically equivalent to a sustained concentration effect, use 5e concentration rules unless the jutsu explicitly replaces them.

Use standard 5e conditions wherever possible before creating custom conditions.

## Damage and durability

Jutsu damage should remain within recognizable 5e scaling unless a named feature establishes otherwise. Narrative destruction does not equal automatic combat damage.

## GM correction rule

If a declared action violates established action economy, resource limits, positioning, reaction economy or known technique rules, the GM must say so before resolving it.

Preferred format:

> "Cool idea. Mechanically that's two actions. You can do X now, or use Y if you have the feature/resource that combines them."

Do not silently allow illegal stacking because it is cinematic.

## Canon vs mechanics

A fictional event can be canon while its exact mechanics remain provisional.

Mark uncertain rules as **DRAFT**, not canon.

Do not retroactively invent modifiers, DCs, damage dice or resource costs merely to make a past scene look cleaner. Record the precedent and formalize the reusable rule going forward.
